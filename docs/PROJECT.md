# Shards Of Light — Project Overview

## Context

**Shards Of Light** is an Android 2D platform game developed as a **VirtueNetz company product**. It was released on Google Play and has a public website.

## My Contribution

I created the game as part of my professional work at VirtueNetz, covering Unity/C# gameplay development, character controls, levels, enemies, bosses, progression, local saving, cinematics, UI, audio integration, mobile optimization, and Android release work.

I generated approximately **30–40% of the game assets**. Other visual/audio materials and tools were sourced from external websites or providers and remain subject to their original licensing terms.

## Product Experience

Rubi journeys through seven frozen-world levels to recover crystal shards. The game combines movement, puzzles, enemies, checkpoints, three-star collectible objectives, and multi-phase boss fights.

## Offline Design

Core gameplay is playable without an internet connection. Level unlocks and completion results are stored locally. Optional advertising and platform services may require network connectivity.

## Release

- **Platform:** Android 8.0+
- **Engine:** Unity 6
- **Status:** Published on Google Play
- **Website:** https://shards-of-light.vercel.app/
- **Google Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.virtuenetz.shardsoflight

## Public Documentation Boundary

This repository documents the finished product and development work without publishing proprietary Unity project files, game source code, third-party media, or private publishing configuration.
