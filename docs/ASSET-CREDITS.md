# Asset Credits & Licensing

Shards Of Light combines self-generated and externally sourced creative materials.

## Asset Origins

- **Approximately 30–40%:** assets generated by the game's developer.
- **Remaining assets:** artwork, music, sound effects, or tools obtained from external websites and providers.

The percentages are an approximate development estimate, **not a statement of copyright ownership or license status**. Generated assets may also be subject to tool or platform terms.
